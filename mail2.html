Ahmedabad Metro • Public Representation

Better Connectivity to Paldi Metro Station

Request for review of the pedestrian route between Paldi Cross Roads and Paldi Metro Station and for keeping the main gate towards Jalaram Temple open.

Thousands of workers, artisans, local residents and daily commuters use the Paldi area for their daily travel.

This representation requests the concerned authorities to review the existing pedestrian access and consider keeping the main gate towards the well-known Jalaram Temple landmark open for improved public convenience and connectivity.
